Privacy Policy for Nearly There

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Nearly There is an app for planning trips: bookings, packing lists and safety at your destination. This page explains what information the app handles and where it is kept.

In short

  • We, the makers of the app, do not collect any information about you.
  • Your trips are stored in your own iCloud and on your phone.
  • The app has no ads, no tracking and no analytics.

What information the app handles

You decide what you enter, for example destinations, dates, bookings (title, times, address, the hotel's or company's contact details and booking reference), travellers' first names or nicknames, packing lists, an emergency card and a small picture per trip.

The app blocks national identity numbers and payment card numbers and never saves them. It never asks for age or date of birth.

Where the information is stored

  • In your iCloud (Apple CloudKit), in the app's private area of your account. Only you, and the people you choose to share a trip with, can see it. Apple's iCloud terms apply.
  • On your phone there is a copy so the app works offline. It is protected by the phone's lock, is excluded from backups and is deleted if a different iCloud account signs in.
  • Settings such as Face ID lock, reminders and Wi-Fi-only sync are stored only on the phone.

Sharing

A trip is shared only with people you invite through iCloud. There are no public links. People you share with can view and edit the trip, and you can stop sharing at any time.

Reading bookings

Text, PDF files, images and boarding passes that you import are interpreted entirely on your phone. On phones with Apple Intelligence, Apple's on-device AI is used. Nothing is sent to us or to any other AI service. The imported material is not saved, only the details you approve.

Attachments

You can choose to save a PDF or image, such as a ticket, as an attachment to a booking. The attachment is stored in your iCloud together with the trip and is shared with the people you share the trip with. A copy is kept on the phone so it can be opened offline. The app checks the file first and does not save it if it appears to contain an identity number or card number. Images are re-saved without location and camera data. The attachment is removed when you delete it, the booking or the trip.

Maps and search

When you search for an address, a hotel or a destination, the search text is sent to Apple Maps to find the place. No other information is sent.

Travel time between two bookings on the same day (walking, public transport and driving) is calculated by Apple Maps. Only the coordinates of the two places are sent, no names, booking references or other details.

Weather

To show the weather at your destination, the destination's coordinates are sent to Apple Weather (WeatherKit). Your phone's own location is not used, and no information about you or your fellow travellers is sent. Weather is only fetched when you open a trip.

Reminders

Reminders are created on the phone and shown only on your phone. They never contain booking references.

Deleting information

Delete a trip in the app and it is removed, with its bookings and packing lists, from iCloud and from the people you shared it with. You can also remove all app data under Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Children

The app is intended for adults planning trips, alone or with others. It does not collect information about children. Children can be added by first name or nickname for the packing list.

Safety at your destination

The app makes no assessment of safety in different countries. General information under "Good to know" may change. Always check current advice from your government's travel advisory service.
